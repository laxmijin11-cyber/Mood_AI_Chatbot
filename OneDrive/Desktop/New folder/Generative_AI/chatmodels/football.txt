The word **"football"** can mean different things depending on where you are in the world, as it refers to several different sports and the balls used to play them. 

Here is a breakdown of the most common meanings:

### 1. Association Football (Soccer)
* **Where the term is used:** Most of the world (Europe, Africa, Asia, South America, etc.).
* **The Sport:** Played between two teams of 11 players with a round ball. The objective is to score by getting the ball into the opposing goal using any part of the body except the hands and arms (unless you are the goalkeeper within your penalty area).
* **The Ball:** A spherical ball, traditionally made of leather panels stitched together, though modern balls are made of advanced synthetic materials. 

### 2. American Football (Gridiron)
* **Where the term is used:** Primarily in the United States and Canada (where it is simply called "football").
* **The Sport:** Played between two teams of 11 players on a rectangular field with goalposts at each end. The objective is to advance an oval ball down the field into the opponent's end zone to score touchdowns. Players can run with the ball or throw it.
* **The Ball:** An oblong, prolate spheroid shape (often nicknamed a "pigskin," though they are made of leather or rubber with pebbled textures for grip) featuring distinct white laces.

### 3. Canadian Football
* Similar to American football, played with a slightly larger ball and specific rule differences in Canada.

### 4. Rugby Football (Rugby Union and Rugby League)
* **Where the term is used:** Popular in the UK, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, France, and parts of the Pacific.
* **The Sport:** A full-contact sport played with an oval ball where players can run with the ball, pass it backward or sideways, and kick it forward to score tries or goals.
* **The Ball:** Oval-shaped, similar to an American football, but generally rounder and plumper at the ends, without laces.

***

**Summary:** 
If you are in the US, a "football" usually means the brown, pointy ball used in American football. If you are almost anywhere else in the world, a "football" means the round ball used to play soccer.
